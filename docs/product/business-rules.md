# Business Rules

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the business rules currently enforced by Revestik.

Business rules describe required system behavior independently from a specific UI implementation.

Rule statuses:
* **Implemented** — currently enforced.
* **In Progress** — implementation has started but is incomplete.
* **Planned** — intended behavior not yet implemented.
* **Deferred** — intentionally postponed.

# 3. Data Integrity

## DAT-001 — Frontend validation is not authoritative

**Status:** Implemented

## DAT-002 — Database constraints protect critical invariants

**Status:** Implemented

## DAT-003 — Persistence entities are not the public HTTP contract

**Status:** Implemented

Shared request/response models define the client/API contract.

## DAT-004 — Critical state transitions must be atomic

**Status:** Implemented

Operations that combine multiple dependent persistence changes must succeed or fail as a single transaction where partial completion would corrupt business state.

Inventory consumption, sale-void inventory restoration, and Purchase-to-Inventory receipt are examples of this rule.

## DAT-005 — Historical operational records should not be physically rewritten without an explicit business rule

**Status:** Implemented

Historical transactions, movements, payments, and inventory provenance are retained where traceability is required.

# 6. Sales

The Sales domain is **Implemented** end to end.

## SAL-001 — A sale belongs to an existing customer

**Status:** Implemented

A Sale references a customer through `CustomerId`.

## SAL-002 — A sale may be created directly or from an issued quotation

**Status:** Implemented

`SourceQuotationId` is optional for direct sales and populated for quotation conversions.

## SAL-003 — Sales support Draft, Issued, and Voided states

**Status:** Implemented

## SAL-004 — A Draft does not have an official VEN number

**Status:** Implemented

The Sale number may remain null while the Sale is Draft.

## SAL-005 — VEN is assigned when the sale is issued

**Status:** Implemented

Format:

```text

VEN-000001

```

The number is generated by the backend through SQL Server-backed sequence infrastructure.

## SAL-006 — VEN generation must remain unique under concurrency

**Status:** Implemented

Concurrent generation is protected by the SQL Server sequence and verified by integration tests.

## SAL-007 — Issued sales store a customer snapshot

**Status:** Implemented

The issued document is not rewritten by later customer changes.

## SAL-008 — Sales support manual and registered-product lines

**Status:** Implemented

`ProductId` is optional.

## SAL-009 — CABYS is optional for the current internal sale workflow

**Status:** Implemented

Direct fiscal-document requirements are outside the current Sales scope.

## SAL-010 — Sales support CRC and USD

**Status:** Implemented

## SAL-011 — Sale monetary calculations are server authoritative

**Status:** Implemented

## SAL-012 — Sale prices use the implemented IVA-inclusive calculation model

**Status:** Implemented

Supported tax rates are currently 0% and 13%.

## SAL-013 — Sales support line discounts and general discounts

**Status:** Implemented

Discount rules prevent invalid values such as percentages over 100% or fixed line discounts exceeding the line gross amount.

## SAL-014 — Sales support additional charges

**Status:** Implemented

## SAL-015 — Issued and voided sales may have an internal PDF

**Status:** Implemented

Draft sales do not expose the official internal sale document.

## SAL-016 — Sales expose paid and outstanding balances

**Status:** Implemented

Balance states are:
* `Pending`
* `PartiallyPaid`
* `Paid`

## SAL-017 — Sales support partial and full payments

**Status:** Implemented

Supported methods currently include:
* Cash
* Sinpe
* BankTransfer
* InternationalTransfer
* Card
* Other

## SAL-018 — Payment history is preserved

**Status:** Implemented

Voiding a payment does not physically delete its historical record.

## SAL-019 — Payment voiding requires a reason

**Status:** Implemented

## SAL-020 — Sale voiding requires a reason

**Status:** Implemented

The sale remains historical as `Voided`.

## SAL-021 — Direct sale voiding must respect active-payment rules

**Status:** Implemented

The workflow prevents a simple sale void while active payments remain unresolved.

## SAL-022 — Corrections use replacement sales

**Status:** Implemented

A correction/replacement does not overwrite the original issued sale.

The original sale is retained as historical and linked to a new replacement Draft.

## SAL-023 — Replacement Drafts are editable

**Status:** Implemented

The new Draft may be reviewed and corrected before issue.

## SAL-024 — A replacement receives a new VEN when issued

**Status:** Implemented

The replacement does not reuse the original VEN.

## SAL-025 — Sales history is searchable and paginated

**Status:** Implemented

Current list behavior supports the implemented search, date, status, balance-status, and currency filters.

## SAL-026 — Sales expose per-currency commercial summaries

**Status:** Implemented

Summary data includes sold, collected, outstanding totals, and sale counts.

## SAL-027 — Sales are internal commercial documents, not fiscal electronic invoices

**Status:** Implemented

Revestik does not currently sign or submit Costa Rican electronic invoices.

## SAL-028 — Issued product-linked sales consume inventory

**Status:** Implemented

When an issued sale line references an inventory-managed product, the corresponding physical inventory quantity is consumed.

Manual sale lines that are not linked to an inventory product do not create inventory movements.

## SAL-029 — Draft sales do not consume inventory

**Status:** Implemented

Inventory is affected by the valid sale-issue operation rather than by Draft creation or editing.

## SAL-030 — Sale inventory consumption uses FIFO

**Status:** Implemented

Available inventory cost layers are consumed in first-in-first-out order.

## SAL-031 — Sale issue may not create negative inventory

**Status:** Implemented

The system does not fabricate negative stock to satisfy a product-linked sale.

If sufficient tracked inventory is not available, the inventory-backed operation must not represent unavailable stock as if it existed.

## SAL-032 — Voiding a sale restores the exact inventory originally consumed

**Status:** Implemented

Sale voiding restores quantities to the same FIFO cost layers consumed by the original sale.

The system does not create an unrelated replacement cost layer for this reversal.

## SAL-033 — Inventory reversal is traceable to the original sale movement

**Status:** Implemented

Reversal inventory movements preserve provenance through the original inventory movement they reverse.

## SAL-034 — Sale inventory reversal is idempotency-protected

**Status:** Implemented

The same original inventory consumption cannot be restored more than once.

If the original consumption state no longer supports a valid reversal, the operation must fail rather than corrupt inventory.

# 7. Inventory

The Inventory core is **Implemented** for the current product stage.

## INV-001 — Inventory represents physical materials only

**Status:** Implemented

Services, transportation, installation, and other commercial charges do not create inventory stock.

## INV-002 — Inventory uses a configurable product catalog

**Status:** Implemented

Products may be organized through configurable categories.

The catalog is intentionally suitable for a relatively small business inventory and does not require an internal SKU for every product.

## INV-003 — CABYS belongs to the product definition but does not uniquely identify a product

**Status:** Implemented

CABYS values may be shared by multiple products.

Product identity is not defined by CABYS alone.

## INV-004 — Inventory and commercial units may differ

**Status:** Implemented

A product may define a physical inventory unit and a commercial unit.

Conversion rules determine how commercial quantities map to physical inventory quantities.

## INV-005 — Whole inventory units are enforced where required

**Status:** Implemented

Products configured as requiring whole physical units cannot persist invalid fractional physical quantities.

Products that legitimately support fractional physical quantities may do so according to their unit configuration.

## INV-006 — Physical stock is the source of truth for inventory availability

**Status:** Implemented

Tracked inventory quantity represents physical availability.

Commercial presentation units do not replace the underlying physical quantity.

## INV-007 — Initial stock creates inventory history

**Status:** Implemented

Initial stock is represented through inventory movements rather than by silently assigning an unexplained quantity.

## INV-008 — Real stock changes create inventory movements

**Status:** Implemented

Stock changes must be represented through traceable inventory movement records.

Inventory history is not reconstructed solely from the current product stock value.

## INV-009 — Inventory movements are historical records

**Status:** Implemented

Persisted inventory movements are retained for traceability and are not treated as disposable current-state rows.

## INV-010 — Inventory cannot become negative

**Status:** Implemented

Tracked physical inventory may not be reduced below zero by a valid inventory operation.

## INV-011 — Manual inventory increases and decreases require explicit operations

**Status:** Implemented

Manual adjustments are represented through dedicated inventory operations and corresponding movement history.

## INV-012 — FIFO is the inventory consumption method

**Status:** Implemented

Inventory decreases that require cost-layer consumption use FIFO.

## INV-013 — Inventory cost layers preserve source cost history

**Status:** Implemented

Cost layers represent the historical cost source associated with inventory quantities.

Existing historical cost values should not be rewritten simply because a newer product cost becomes available.

## INV-014 — Product CurrentCost is a current reference, not a rewrite of historical cost

**Status:** Implemented

The product's current cost may reflect a current or latest known cost reference.

It does not replace the historical unit cost attached to existing inventory layers.

## INV-015 — Unknown inventory cost may exist explicitly

**Status:** Implemented

Inventory quantity may exist with an unknown historical unit cost where the original cost was unavailable.

Unknown cost must remain visible rather than being silently replaced with an invented value.

## INV-016 — Unknown inventory costs may be resolved explicitly

**Status:** Implemented

An unknown cost layer may receive a resolved cost through the dedicated cost-resolution workflow.

The original `UnitCost` source remains unchanged.

Resolved cost information is stored separately together with resolution metadata.

## INV-017 — Effective inventory cost uses the original cost when available

**Status:** Implemented

Where an original `UnitCost` exists, it remains authoritative for that cost layer.

A resolved cost is used only when the original historical cost was unknown.

## INV-018 — Physical counts are explicit inventory operations

**Status:** Implemented

Physical inventory counts are represented as dedicated records rather than directly overwriting product stock without history.

## INV-019 — Physical-count differences create traceable adjustments

**Status:** Implemented

When counted stock differs from system stock, the resulting adjustment is represented through inventory movement history linked to the physical count.

## INV-020 — Physical-count updates respect lifecycle state

**Status:** Implemented

Physical counts have explicit workflow status and cannot be mutated arbitrarily once their lifecycle no longer permits the requested change.

## INV-021 — Product deactivation does not destroy history

**Status:** Implemented

A discontinued product remains persisted and may continue to appear in historical records.

## INV-022 — A discontinued product may be reactivated

**Status:** Implemented

Reactivation is available while the product has not been permanently archived from normal product workflows.

## INV-023 — “Permanent deletion” in the product UI is logical archival

**Status:** Implemented

The user-facing permanent-delete action does not physically remove the product row from the database.

Instead:

```text

IsDeleted = true

IsArchived = true

```

The product is permanently removed from normal product workflows while historical records and references remain preserved.

## INV-024 — An active product must be discontinued before permanent archival

**Status:** Implemented

A product must first enter the discontinued state before the permanent archival action is accepted.

## INV-025 — Archived products are excluded from normal product listings

**Status:** Implemented

Archived products do not appear in Active, Discontinued, or All normal product catalog views.

## INV-026 — Archived products cannot be reactivated through the normal product workflow

**Status:** Implemented

Permanent archival is intentionally final from the normal product-management perspective.

## INV-027 — Historical references do not block archival

**Status:** Implemented

A discontinued product may be archived even when it has historical sales, quotations, inventory movements, cost layers, or physical-count references.

Archival preserves those records rather than deleting them.

## INV-028 — Product-linked sale entry uses registered product inventory information

**Status:** Implemented

When a sale line is linked to a registered product, inventory identity and quantity conversion rules come from the registered product.

## INV-029 — Product-linked sale values protect inventory consistency

**Status:** Implemented

Inventory-relevant product information used by the sale workflow is not treated as arbitrary free-form inventory data.

## INV-030 — Inventory operations requiring multiple related changes are atomic

**Status:** Implemented

Operations such as FIFO consumption, sale reversal, physical-count adjustment, and other critical transitions must not leave partially applied inventory state.

## INV-031 — One physical inventory location is currently assumed

**Status:** Implemented

The current Inventory domain represents one warehouse/stock location.

Multi-warehouse allocation is not part of the current implementation.

## INV-032 — Purchase processing is not owned by Inventory

**Status:** Implemented

Inventory owns physical stock behavior.

Supplier records, Purchase lifecycle, Accounts Payable, received Electronic Documents, email ingestion, and XML parsing remain outside the Inventory domain.

Inventory receives valid stock-entry effects through explicit Purchase receipt operations.

# 8. Purchases, Electronic Documents, and Gmail

## SUP-001 — Supplier names are unique

**Status:** Implemented

Supplier names are unique in the Supplier domain.

Product names are not treated as a global unique inventory identity rule.

## PUR-001 — Purchases own supplier and purchase workflows

**Status:** Implemented

The Purchases domain owns supplier association, Purchase records, Purchase lines, cash/credit conditions, due dates, Purchase payments, Accounts Payable behavior, and supplier Purchase history.

## PUR-002 — A Purchase does not automatically modify Inventory

**Status:** Implemented

Creating or editing a Purchase does not silently mutate stock.

Inventory effects occur through the explicit Purchase receipt workflow.

## PUR-003 — Purchase receipt produces traceable Inventory effects

**Status:** Implemented

Valid product-linked Purchase receipt creates traceable Inventory movements and FIFO cost layers using the Purchase cost information.

## PUR-004 — A Purchase cannot be applied to Inventory twice

**Status:** Implemented

Duplicate Inventory application from the same Purchase is prevented.

## PUR-005 — Purchase/AP critical transitions are atomic where required

**Status:** Implemented

Operations that coordinate multiple dependent Purchase, payment, Accounts Payable, or Inventory changes must succeed or fail consistently when partial completion would corrupt business state.

## EDOC-001 — Purchase and Electronic Document are different concepts

**Status:** Implemented

A `Purchase` is an operational business record.

An `ElectronicDocument` is a received fiscal document.

Receiving, accepting, or classifying an Electronic Document does not automatically create a Purchase.

## EDOC-002 — Electronic Documents do not automatically modify Inventory

**Status:** Implemented

Inventory effects must be produced by explicit operational workflows rather than merely by importing or accepting fiscal XML.

## EDOC-003 — Received XML is quarantined before canonical acceptance

**Status:** Implemented

XML received manually or through Gmail enters the received-document staging/quarantine workflow before becoming a canonical Electronic Document.

## EDOC-004 — Duplicate fiscal documents are not independent economic facts

**Status:** Implemented

A duplicate fiscal document must not be persisted as a second accepted business event.

Duplicate detection is part of the received-document ingestion boundary.

## EDOC-005 — Hacienda document recognition and business processing are separate

**Status:** Implemented

A Hacienda XML 4.4 document family may be recognized and structurally valid even when Revestik does not currently enable business processing for that family.

Recognized-but-not-enabled documents must not be mislabeled as malformed XML.

## EDOC-006 — Secure XML validation uses local schemas

**Status:** Implemented

Received fiscal XML is validated using the implemented local Hacienda XSD platform.

DTD processing and unsafe external resource resolution are not allowed.

Schemas are not downloaded dynamically at runtime.

## EDOC-007 — `FechaEmision` is the economic date

**Status:** Implemented

The economic period of a received Electronic Document is determined by its `FechaEmision`.

Import time, Gmail synchronization time, quarantine time, and manual acceptance time do not replace `FechaEmision` for historical period reporting.

## EDOC-008 — History defaults to the current calendar month

**Status:** Implemented

Opening Received Documents History defaults `DateFrom` to the first day and `DateTo` to the last day of the current calendar month.

Clearing History filters returns to this current-month period.

## EDOC-009 — Historical date ranges are supported

**Status:** Implemented

Users may explicitly select another valid `DateFrom` / `DateTo` range for Received Documents History.

## EDOC-010 — Invalid historical date ranges are rejected

**Status:** Implemented

A date range where `DateFrom > DateTo` is invalid.

The backend validates the requested period.

## EDOC-011 — Pending review remains global

**Status:** Implemented

The Pending received-document queue is not restricted to the current History month.

Older documents that still require review remain visible while Pending.

## EDOC-012 — Period summaries follow the selected economic period

**Status:** Implemented

Received-document period summaries use the selected `FechaEmision` date range.

The period summary is intentionally not narrowed by free-text search, processing-status filtering, or accounting-category filtering applied to the History list.

## EDOC-013 — Accounting classification dimensions remain separate

**Status:** Implemented

CAByS, accounting category, accounting nature, operational destination, and payment condition are separate business concepts.

Automated suggestions remain reviewable and editable.

## EDOC-014 — Line-level classification may override document-level classification

**Status:** Implemented

When line-level overrides exist, the implemented classification and summary behavior respects the line-specific allocation instead of blindly treating the entire document as one category/destination.

## GMAIL-001 — Gmail is a transport mechanism only

**Status:** Implemented

Gmail provides an external message/attachment transport path into the received-document workflow.

It does not directly create Purchases or Inventory movements.

## GMAIL-002 — Gmail access is read-only

**Status:** Implemented

The current integration uses the `gmail.readonly` OAuth scope.

## GMAIL-003 — Gmail-derived XML uses the same validation/quarantine boundary

**Status:** Implemented

XML retrieved through Gmail must pass the same recognition, validation, duplicate handling, and quarantine workflow as manual XML input.

## GMAIL-004 — Manual Gmail synchronization is single-flight

**Status:** Implemented

Overlapping synchronization runs are prevented.

## GMAIL-005 — Duplicate and rejected attachments are tracked

**Status:** Implemented

Repeated mailbox discovery must not create duplicate accepted fiscal documents.

Rejected attachments may use the implemented controlled retry behavior.

## GMAIL-006 — Gmail synchronization is manual

**Status:** Implemented / intentional

The current Gmail integrations synchronize only on explicit user request.

Background periodic synchronization is intentionally deferred. Manual synchronization
must preserve read-only access, duplicate safety, retry behavior, and single-flight
protection.

## GMAIL-007 — Bank-voucher Gmail sync must remain retryable

**Status:** Implemented

The voucher integration filters the mailbox to the configured sender and subject,
uses Gmail message id as its duplicate identity, and does not advance the last
successful synchronization cursor when a run contains per-message failures.

Persistent Gmail rate-limit errors stop the synchronization rather than producing
hundreds of independent failed-message results.

## Accounts Receivable Expansion

**Status:** Planned / optional expansion

Current Sales already support payment and outstanding-balance tracking.

A broader receivables domain may be introduced if business requirements expand into aging, collection workflows, or customer-level aggregation.

## Expenses

**Status:** Implemented

Manual expenses require a name, description, positive amount, and expense date.

Bank vouchers are payment evidence and are not fiscal invoices. Their review states
are `NeedsReview`, `Accepted`, `Matched`, and `Ignored`.

Only `Accepted` vouchers contribute independently to consolidated expense totals.
`Matched` vouchers are represented economically by their linked fiscal document and
must not be counted again. `NeedsReview` and `Ignored` vouchers do not contribute.

Processed Electronic Documents contribute when their effective accounting nature is
`OperatingExpense`. Line-level accounting classification may override the document
classification for reporting purposes.

Manual expenses are CRC. Electronic-document and voucher currencies are preserved
and reported separately; no implicit currency conversion is performed.

## Reports

**Status:** Planned

Reports are expected to aggregate information owned by operational domains.

Expected reporting areas include:

* Purchase totals.
* Sales totals.
* Inventory value.
* Purchase-versus-sales comparisons.
* Period-based summaries.
* Received Electronic Document summaries.
* Other operational totals required by the business.

Reports should not become the source of transactional truth.

## Dashboard and Analytics

**Status:** Planned

The Dashboard/Main area will present important current-period information derived from operational domains.

It should not own transactional business rules.

## Public RevestikCR.com Website

**Status:** Planned

A public customer-facing site is planned separately from the internal operational application.

Its purpose may include completed projects, company information, and other public commercial content.

# 9. Deferred Integrations

## Electronic Invoicing

**Status:** Deferred

Direct integration with Costa Rica's Ministerio de Hacienda is intentionally postponed.

Internal `VEN` documents remain separate from outbound fiscal electronic invoices and from received Electronic Documents.

## Automated Outbound Email and WhatsApp Distribution

**Status:** Deferred

Automated outbound document distribution is not required for the current internal workflow.

## AI-Assisted Expense Ingestion

**Status:** Deferred

AI-assisted extraction and classification may be introduced later for documents where deterministic structured data is unavailable or insufficient.

Where structured XML exists, deterministic parsing should be preferred.

## Test-Suite Performance Optimization

**Status:** Deferred

The current suite prioritizes regression protection and correctness.

Performance optimization may be revisited after the main application and public RevestikCR.com work are further completed or when deployment creates a practical need for faster feedback.

# 10. Rule Change Process

A business-rule change may require updates to:
1. This document.
2. Shared request or response contracts.
3. Server-side validation.
4. Application services.
5. EF Core configuration.
6. Database migrations.
7. Automated tests.
8. Client-side validation or UI behavior.
9. API documentation.

Not every rule requires changes at every layer.

# 11. Source of Truth

This document describes expected business behavior.

The executable application and database determine the behavior actually enforced by the current codebase.

If documentation and implementation disagree, the discrepancy must be investigated rather than assuming either side is automatically correct.
